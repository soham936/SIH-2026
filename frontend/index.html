Maitri Station
Schirmacher Oasis, Queen Maud Land, Antarctica

India's second Antarctic station (est. 1989), operational year-round. Hub for glaciology, geology, atmospheric science, and remote sensing research.

Lat/Lon
70°45'S, 11°44'E
Elevation
~117 m ASL
Established
1989
Winter Crew
~25 personnel
🔬
Bharati Station
Larsemann Hills, East Antarctica

India's modern, sustainable Antarctic station (est. 2012). Focuses on marine science, biology, oceanography, and atmospheric studies with international partnerships.

Lat/Lon
69°24'S, 76°11'E
Status
Fully operational
Established
2012
Focus
Marine & Ocean
🧭
Himadri Station
Ny-Ålesund, Svalbard, Norway (Arctic)

India's only Arctic research station (est. 2008), co-located with 11 other nations in the world's northernmost research settlement. Studies Arctic amplification and glaciology.

Lat/Lon
78°55'N, 11°56'E
Season
Summer (Apr–Oct)
Established
2008
Partner
Kings Bay AS
